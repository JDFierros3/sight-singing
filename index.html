Chord Quality
A chord's "quality" is its emotional color: major sounds bright, minor sounds darker, diminished sounds tense. Select a root, quality, and inversion to hear and see it on the staff. The drone lets you feel the chord against your voice.

Hidden Cluster
Polyphony training: you'll hear 2-3 pitches played simultaneously. Identify each pitch by ear — hum them out one at a time. Use Reveal only after you've committed to an answer. This builds the harmonic hearing needed for SATB singing.

Interval Training
You'll hear two pitches in sequence. Identify the second pitch relative to the first using solfege (e.g., "Do to Sol" = a perfect fifth). Press Reveal only after you decide — guessing before listening doesn't build the skill.
